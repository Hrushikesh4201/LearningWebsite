🌐 Digital Life Skills

Learn the essential digital skills required for everyday life, education, and the workplace.

Course Introduction

Digital Life Skills help people use computers, the Internet, mobile phones, and online services safely and effectively. These skills are important for students, professionals, and anyone using digital technology.

1. What are Digital Life Skills?

Digital Life Skills are the knowledge and abilities needed to use computers, mobile devices, the Internet, email, and digital applications confidently and safely.

2. Why are Digital Skills Important?
  • Online Learning
  • Job Opportunities
  • Communication
  • Online Banking
  • Government Services
  • Digital Payments
3. Basic Digital Skills
  • Using a Computer
  • Using the Internet
  • Creating an Email Account
  • Sending Emails
  • Downloading Files
  • Uploading Files
  • Using Search Engines
4. Internet Safety

Always use strong passwords, avoid clicking unknown links, never share OTPs, and use trusted websites while browsing.

5. Advantages of Digital Technology
  • Fast Communication
  • Easy Access to Information
  • Online Education
  • Digital Payments
  • Remote Work
6. Disadvantages
  • Cyber Crime
  • Privacy Risks
  • Screen Addiction
  • Fake News
  • Online Scams
7. Summary

In this course you learned about Digital Life Skills, their importance, basic Internet usage, online safety, advantages, and disadvantages of digital technology.
